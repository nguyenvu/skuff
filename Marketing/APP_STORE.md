### Description

```
Skuff keeps your apps in folder tabs along the edge of the screen.

Spotlight is for typing a name you already know. Skuff is for looking. Short colored tabs stay out of the way until you want a group of apps, then that folder slides out in the same color as its tab.

Click a tab to open it. Click again, click outside, or press Esc to close it. Skuff does not take over the window you were typing in, and it does not put an icon in the Dock.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Put the strip on the left, right, top, or bottom. On the bottom it sits just above the Dock and matches its width.
• On first launch, Skuff files your installed apps into tabs by category, such as Dev, Office, and Design.
• Search inside a folder, pin favorites to the top, and see a green dot on apps that are already running.
• Hover a tab to peek at a few icons. Drag tabs to reorder them. Drag the grip to slide the strip. Each display remembers its own place.
• Drop an app from Finder onto a tab. Press Option-1 through Option-9 to open a tab from anywhere.
• Tabs shrink and overlap when the edge gets crowded. The open tab stays readable.
• The strip hides while another app is full screen.

MAKE IT YOURS
Name a tab, pick a color, and add an emoji or an SF Symbol. Choose a flat color, a gradient, or the system glass. Pastel is included. Labels switch between dark and light ink so they stay readable.

FREE
Five app tabs, colors, emoji, symbols, search, shortcuts, and the standard tab shape. A sixth tab shows an upgrade card inside that folder only. Nothing else is interrupted.

SKUFF PRO
A one-time purchase. Unlimited tabs, every theme, custom shapes, your own font, and PNG or SVG icons. Family Sharing is included. Restore Purchases is in Settings.

Export your setup to a file and import it on another Mac.

Skuff supports VoiceOver and Reduce Motion.
```

## App Review notes

Dán vào Notes for Review. App là accessory: không có icon Dock, không có menu bar.

```
Skuff is a menu-bar-less accessory. After launch, colored folder tabs appear on the right edge of the screen. There is no Dock icon.

How to review:
1. Launch Skuff. Tabs are created automatically from installed apps.
2. Click a tab. A folder of apps slides out. Click an app to open it. Press Esc to close the folder.
3. Right-click a tab and choose Settings to open preferences.
4. Settings → General includes Upgrade and Restore Purchases.
5. The In-App Purchase product ID is no.voq.skuff.pro (non-consumable). A screenshot of the upgrade card is attached.
6. Free allows 5 app tabs. Creating more shows a locked folder with the purchase button. It does not block the rest of the app.

The app needs no special permission prompts. It reads installed applications to build the first set of tabs.
```
